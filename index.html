The Credit Gap

Good businesses, thin files.

Most South African SMEs run on discipline that traditional credit bureau reports miss — consistent cash flow, supplier reliability, and repeat customers. Aeonium Creda™ closes this visibility gap.

01

Invisible to Lenders

Owner-run businesses are routinely declined for lacking formal paperwork legacy scoring demands — regardless of actual performance.

02

Static vs. Behavioural

Bureau scores record a single snapshot. They miss the operational rhythm and week-to-week resilience that proves true repayment capacity.

03

Slow Underwriting

Manual paperwork drags decision timelines out for weeks — long after the working-capital opportunity has passed.

Aeonium Creda™ Engine

From operating behavior to a fair credit decision.
