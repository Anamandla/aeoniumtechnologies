The Gap

Good businesses, thin files.

Most South African SMEs run on discipline that never shows up in a credit bureau report — consistent cash flow, reliable suppliers, repeat customers. Traditional lending was never built to see that. Aeonium Creda™ was.

01

Invisible to lenders

Owner-run and informal businesses are routinely declined for lacking the formal documentation legacy scoring demands — regardless of how the business actually performs.

02

Static, not behavioural

Bureau scores capture a moment, not a pattern. They miss the week-to-week resilience that actually predicts whether a business will repay.

03

Slow to decide

Manual underwriting drags financing timelines out for weeks — long after the working-capital need that prompted the application has passed.

Aeonium Creda™

One engine, from raw behaviour to a lending decision.

Creda turns everyday operating signals into a transparent, explainable credit profile — built for lenders who want more confidence, and SMEs who deserve a fairer look.

01 / Ingest

Connect the data

Transaction flow, supplier activity and operating history are pulled in securely with the business owner's consent.

02 / Read

Model the behaviour

Pattern-recognition models translate raw activity into indicators of consistency, resilience and repayment capacity.

03 / Score

Generate the score

A behavioural credit score is produced with a plain-language explanation of what drove it — for the lender and the business.

04 / Decide

Faster decisions

Once live, lenders will act on a score they can trust and explain — cutting decision time from weeks to days.
